Standing correction — gsm8k

Two earlier reports said gsm8k was losing accuracy at big budgets. That was wrong, and it was my measurement rather than the model.

The grader wants the answer after ####. The model writes it in shapes the grader rejects: 13  \n####, 34####, ####  \n11, ####\n\n5. Taking the last number in the response instead agrees with the grader on every answer it marks correct and rescues hundreds more. On that measure gsm8k scores about 0.91 at every budget, flat for the whole run. There is no gsm8k problem.

10 — Stage B readiness

Yes, start it — but change two numbers first

Stage A exists to teach the number-to-length mapping. It has. Correlation 0.933, on target 78.4%, median ratio 1.00×, and accuracy up on all three sets. There is little left for it to teach.

Stage B needs three things from its starting checkpoint, and all three are in place:

  • The tag is closed. Stage B multiplies, so a malformed answer scores zero whatever its length. Well-formed is 99.9% at step 480, and hit 100% at step 440.
  • No collapse. 0% empty think blocks and 0% floor failures at the last three evals. Stage B has a collapse-to-short attractor of its own, so this matters.
  • The mapping is learned. That is the whole point of Stage A, and it is done.

Checkpoint global_step_480 is on disk and is the one to start from.

But Stage B as configured would barely do anything

I replayed the current rollouts through Stage B's reward. Almost every row lands at a multiplier of about 0.5 — which means Stage B would roughly halve every reward and change almost nothing about length.

Stage B length multiplier if it started from this checkpoint. 1.00 keeps the full task score, 0.00 wipes it. Training rollouts, steps 441–481.

The cause is the grace zone. Stage B's multiplier is clip(α·(budget − n) + 0.5, 0, 1), so it only leaves 0.5 when α·(budget − n) is large. That takes 0.5/α tokens of error — 3,125 tokens for math, 2,083 for ifeval, 1,351 for chat.

The model now lands within a few hundred tokens of its budget. So the term barely moves. At the ~500 rung math writes 1.34× its budget and still keeps 46% of its score — no real pressure to tighten.

Your own note in lcpo.py anticipates exactly this: "if they do not converge, switch the term to (budget − n_think) / budget, which gives the same grace zone in percentage terms at every scale." That is now the right call. With a relative term, being 34% over costs something at every budget size instead of nothing at small ones.

Second number: math's length signal nearly vanishes under Stage B

Stage B multiplies task score by the length term, so a row scoring zero carries no length signal at all. Math only gets 26.7% of its rows right, against 82% for chat and 93% for ifeval. That is why math's length share of the within-group signal falls to 5.6%.

Overall the within-group reward spread roughly halves going from Stage A to Stage B (math 0.216 → 0.098). That is expected — Stage B refines rather than teaches — but math will get almost nothing out of it. Worth deciding up front whether that is acceptable or whether math wants a larger α in Stage B.

Other things worth knowing

  • Fix the gsm8k grader. It marks about half of correct gsm8k answers wrong, so your live dashboard cannot see that gsm8k is fine. Accepting the last number in the response fixes it.
  • MATH-500's 256 rung sits at 0.68 against 0.79 at the start. That is a real limit — a hard maths problem in 256 tokens is harder — not something α will fix.
  • Entropy is still climbing, 1.38 → 1.79. Everything else is stable, but a policy that keeps getting less certain is worth an eye in Stage B.
